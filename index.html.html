Cyber Scam Detector

A web app that checks suspicious messages and links for phishing, so anyone can find out in seconds whether something is a scam before they click.

“Your bank account is blocked. Verify now: http://secure-bank-login.example”

Looks suspicious: do not click
Open the live app

The problem

Phishing messages and fake links reach people every day by SMS, WhatsApp and email. Most people have no quick way to check them.

What it does

  • Takes a pasted message or link and checks it for phishing signs.
  • Gives a clear verdict in plain language.
  • Installs on a phone as an app (named ScamCheck) and works on mobile screens.
